# Benchmarks

Nothing ships untested against the field. ferroplan runs differentially
against reference planners over a curated subset of the IPC contest suites —
classical (gripper, blocks, logistics, …), numeric (rovers, satellite,
depots, …), ADL, and IPC-5 simple-preferences. Coverage, plan validity, and
speed are checked against the C **Metric-FF**; IPC-5 **preference quality is
scored against SGPlan5**, the contest winner (per-instance metrics pulled
from the official archive — see the
[scoreboard](https://github.com/seanchatmangpt/ferroplan/blob/main/benchmarks/ipc5-scoreboard.md)).
A locally-built SGPlan6 stands in for solvability/validity agreement.

The harness lives in [`benchmarks/`](https://github.com/seanchatmangpt/ferroplan/tree/main/benchmarks):
solvability agreement, plan validity, plan length, and — for preferences —
metric comparison, plus `criterion` micro-benchmarks running parse/ground/search
under the clock.

Full-corpus scoreboards over IPC-2008/2011, every plan VAL-validated, sit alongside:
[seq-sat](https://github.com/seanchatmangpt/ferroplan/blob/main/benchmarks/ipc67-results.md),
[tempo-sat](https://github.com/seanchatmangpt/ferroplan/blob/main/benchmarks/ipc67-temporal.md),
and [net-benefit](https://github.com/seanchatmangpt/ferroplan/blob/main/benchmarks/ipc67-netben.md) —
regenerated every release by `benchmarks/ipc67.py`. The game track keeps its
own ledger: [`bazaar-thinks.md`](https://github.com/seanchatmangpt/ferroplan/blob/main/benchmarks/bazaar-thinks.md)
— think latency/quality curves, the live multi-mind loop, generated by the
`bazaar_thinks` / `bazaar_live` examples.

Curated-subset results tables are committed and published here as-is. They're
machine-dependent — your hardware, your numbers. Regenerate locally per
`benchmarks/COMPARING.md`.
